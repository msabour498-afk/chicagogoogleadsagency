Google Ads for remodeling contractors in Chicago

Kitchen, bath, and basement remodels are big decisions. People research for weeks or months, collect several quotes, and only some of them have the budget for the job they're picturing. The account has to attract serious homeowners, and the tracking has to follow leads all the way to signed contracts.

What's different about this industry

  • Project-level campaignsKitchens, bathrooms, basements, and additions each get their own campaign, ads, and landing page.
  • Lead qualificationLanding pages and forms can ask about project type and timing, so you spend time on the right leads.
  • Long sales cyclesSigned contracts are sent back to Google Ads from your CRM, so it learns which leads become jobs, even weeks later.
  • Showing your workImage assets with photos of your own projects, if you have them, to stand out in the results.
