Performance Max, set up with guardrails

Performance Max lets Google place your ads across Search, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover, and Maps from a single campaign. It can work well, but only when it is fed accurate conversion data and the right signals. Left alone, it tends to spend money where it's easiest, not where it's most valuable.

What we do

  • Conversion goals firstWe make sure the campaign is optimizing for real leads or sales, not page views or button clicks.
  • Asset groupsHeadlines, images, videos, and descriptions organized by service or audience so the ads stay relevant.
  • Audience signalsCustomer lists, website visitors, and search themes that point the campaign toward the right people.
  • Brand and placement controlsBrand exclusions and negative keywords where available, so it isn't taking credit for people already searching your name.
  • Ongoing reviewWe check where spend is going and adjust when results drift.

Is it a fit?

Performance Max is a fit when you have solid conversion tracking in place and enough budget for the campaign to learn. We'll tell you plainly if your account isn't ready for it yet.
